The projects share one approach: learn from sparse, outcome-based rewards in fast simulators, with curricula over start states, and measure the result against strong fixed baselines.

Projects

Terminal: a self-play Rocket League bot

Terminal, a bot trained by self-play in RocketSim. Plays 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3; entered in the RLBot Championship 2026. Interactive replays, clips and results.

clothrl: manipulation from a sparse reward

A Franka arm learns to insert a cork into a jar, stack cubes and drape a cloth, from a sparse success reward in GPU-parallel physics. Toward pressing a cork through a cloth.
