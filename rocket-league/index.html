Abstract

Terminal is a Rocket League bot trained entirely by self-play reinforcement learning in a physics simulator (RocketSim). It plays 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3, and was entered in the RLBot Championship 2026. The replays, clips and numbers below come from our latest checkpoints, playing in simulation.

Replay viewer

Four full 5-minute games, recorded in simulation. Terminal is blue and attacks the goal on the right of the default view. The scene is drawn procedurally in your browser; no game assets are used. Space plays or pauses, arrow keys seek, N / P jump to the next / previous goal, C, D or 0-5 switch camera (Director picks the action like a replay director), drag to orbit.
