RLBot Championship 2026 entry

Terminal

Terminal is a Rocket League bot trained entirely by self-play reinforcement learning in a physics simulator (RocketSim). It plays 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3, and was entered in the RLBot Championship 2026. The replays, clips and numbers below come from our latest checkpoints, playing in simulation.
